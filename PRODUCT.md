# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, one story. Primary: developers and product teams at platforms that need to trust credentials — ATS/HRIS, hiring tools, fintechs, marketplaces, communities — who integrate verification through an API/SDK. Secondary: institutions that issue credentials — universities, bootcamps, academies, certification bodies, event organizers — who need issuance that is verifiable anywhere and painless for recipients. Investors will see this page, but it is deliberately written for the market, not for them (founder's explicit instruction).

## Product Purpose

Glemo is a credential verification network: infrastructure that makes any credential instantly verifiable and unforgeable. Issuers create tamper-proof credentials in minutes; any relying party verifies them in one API call — from any issuer, without contacting them, forever. Success looks like design partners on both sides of the network (issuers and verifiers) and developers shipping their first verification in an afternoon.

## Positioning

The neutral verification layer for the world's credentials: one integration to verify anything, from anyone, instantly — with zero crypto knowledge required.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: "Start building" (developer early access / API keys).
- Secondary CTA: "Book a demo" (institutions, sales-led).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Credentials that prove themselves."
- Belief ladder: (1) In the AI era, anyone can fake a credential — trusting PDFs is broken. (2) Verifying today is manual, slow (days) and unreliable. (3) Glemo verifies any credential in one API call, instantly. (4) It's built on open standards, with no lock-in and no crypto complexity for anyone. (5) I can start now (dev) or talk to the team (institution).
- Proof on hand: pre-launch — no client logos or testimonials yet, and none will be fabricated. Proof comes from: open standards compliance (W3C Verifiable Credentials, Open Badges 3.0), the live product demo on the page itself, and the design-partner program invitation. Product-property stats only (latency, steps, standards); never invented usage numbers.

## Brand Personality

Precise, fluid, incorruptible. The voice is sober and direct — zero crypto jargon, zero hype adjectives, no exclamation marks. The page should evoke instant technical credibility ("this is serious infrastructure") and quiet confidence; the wow comes from motion craft and the product demonstrating itself, never from claims.

## Anti-references

- Crypto-dashboard aesthetic: neon mint + purple + red on flat black (the old Glemo palette). The word "blockchain" never appears on the page.
- Friendly-pastel SaaS (Certifier): cartoon illustrations, 17 redundant sections, "#1" claims.
- Dated enterprise (Accredible): Montserrat 800, magenta CTAs, stock photos of smiling people, static pages.
- Investor-pitch page: no TAM slides, no "backed by" theater.
- Generic AI landing grammar: eyebrow-kicker above every section, purple-blue gradients, gradient text, identical card grids, fake metrics.

## Design Principles

1. Blockchain invisible. Sell the outcome (instant, unforgeable verification) — never the technology underneath. The motion tech is invisible too: effects serve the story of data flowing and being verified.
2. Show, don't claim. The product verifies live on the page (network canvas, terminal demo). Demonstration beats adjectives; honest product properties beat invented numbers.
3. The verified moment is the brand. The green pulse/check — the "verification stroke" — is the single ritual every section builds toward. It is the only thing that glows.
4. Two doors, one house. Developers and institutions each find their path (dual CTA, audience sections) inside one coherent narrative — never two disjointed pages.
5. Craft is the credibility. A page about trust must itself be impeccable: 60fps, AA contrast, reduced-motion respected, EN/ES parity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast throughout (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text/UI). `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere: canvas animation pauses to a static frame, reveals become instant, marquees stop. Fully keyboard-navigable; focus-visible styles on all interactive elements. Bilingual EN (default) + ES with full copy parity.
